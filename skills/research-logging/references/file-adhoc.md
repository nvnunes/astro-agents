# Ad Hoc Material Instructions

Use entry-local `adhoc/` for notes, exploratory scripts, and artifacts that the
researcher wants to keep without admitting them to the recorded workflow.
Preserve the material as encountered unless the researcher requests changes.

Do not apply production-script checks, record its commands, declare its inputs
or outputs, create evidence or retention metadata for it, validate it, or
include it in reproduction.

Entry documents may use ordinary entry-relative Markdown links to `adhoc/`
material. Do not attach evidence markers to those links. Place them under
`Background:`, `Observations:`, or an existing or explicitly authorized
`Decisions:` label, never under `Steps:` or `Results:`.

Ad hoc material may supplement context but must not independently support a
recorded result or observation.
