# Citation Support

Assess whether each material statement attributed to an external reference
accurately represents what that reference establishes. Review the attributed
statement together with its cited source and the context needed to interpret
both.

## Inspect

Use the statement and surrounding research context, citation and reference
record, cited source, and directly relevant passages, figures, tables,
appendices, or supplementary material.

## Review Criteria

- Check whether the cited source contains or establishes the attributed
  proposition.
- Compare the population, conditions, scope, time, and qualifications in the
  source with the statement in the log.
- Preserve the source's distinction between observation, interpretation,
  hypothesis, and conclusion.
- Check whether the source supports the statement's quantitative or qualitative
  specificity.
- Distinguish an inference made by the research account from a conclusion made
  by the cited source.

Assess each distinct attributed proposition. When one source is cited
repeatedly for the same proposition, inspect it once and reuse that assessment
unless the context changes the meaning.

## Exclusions

- Mechanical checks own citation syntax, resolvable identifiers, and authored
  reference metadata.
- Source Authority owns whether the cited source is trustworthy and fit for
  use.
- Claim Support owns whether the account's complete evidentiary basis supports
  its claim.
- Claim Calibration owns the strength and scope of the account's wording.

## Finding Guidance

- **Issue:** A citation materially misstates, overextends, or fails to support
  the proposition attributed to it.
- **Improvement:** The attribution is defensible, but a more precise passage,
  qualification, or primary reference would make the support clearer.
- **Unverified:** The source or necessary portion cannot be accessed or
  interpreted reliably.
