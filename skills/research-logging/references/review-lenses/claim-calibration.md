# Claim Calibration

Assess whether a claim's certainty, scope, generality, and treatment of
uncertainty match the strength and limits of its support.

## Inspect

Use the claim, nearby qualifications, supporting evidence and reasoning,
relevant uncertainty, methodological limits, and contrary or unresolved
material in the reviewed account.

## Review Criteria

- Check whether the wording expresses an appropriate degree of certainty.
- Limit the claim to the populations, conditions, configurations, and time span
  actually supported.
- Distinguish descriptive, predictive, causal, and mechanistic conclusions.
- Check whether uncertainty, preliminary status, negative results, and material
  caveats appear where they change the claim's meaning.
- Identify unsupported generalization from a local result.

## Exclusions

- Claim Support owns whether the proposition is warranted at all.
- Statistical Validity owns the inference itself; this lens owns how its
  strength and uncertainty are expressed.
- Research-Log Writing owns clarity and organization rather than evidentiary
  calibration.

## Finding Guidance

- **Issue:** Wording materially overstates certainty, scope, causality,
  generality, or resolution, or understates a conclusion in a way that distorts
  the research account.
- **Improvement:** A more precise qualification would help without correcting a
  materially misleading claim.
- **Unverified:** The support or uncertainty needed to judge calibration cannot
  be established.
