# Cross-Entry Consistency

Assess whether materially related entries agree about terminology, units,
configurations, assumptions, results, and lifecycle state, or explain their
differences clearly.

## Inspect

Use related entries, retained evidence and artifacts, configuration and method
descriptions, cross-references, lifecycle statements, and the maintained
summary when it helps identify the intended current state.

## Review Criteria

- Compare repeated terms and quantities for compatible meanings and units.
- Check whether differences in configuration, assumptions, populations, or
  conditions are explicit where they affect comparison.
- Compare repeated or overlapping results within their stated conditions and
  uncertainty.
- Check whether contradictions, corrections, and superseded findings are
  identified rather than silently coexisting.
- Check whether lifecycle state is consistent across entry links, prose, and
  later work.

Group entries by research topic, quantity, or configuration rather than
comparing every entry indiscriminately or relying only on chronology.

## Exclusions

- Do not require uniform conclusions from experiments that differ materially;
  require the differences to be real and intelligible.
- Summary Fidelity owns the maintained summary's representation of entries.
- Presentation Consistency owns figure and table conventions rather than
  factual agreement.
- Research-Log Conformance owns adherence to lifecycle and navigation
  conventions.

## Finding Guidance

- **Issue:** Entries materially contradict one another, use incompatible
  definitions or conditions without disclosure, or leave lifecycle state
  ambiguous.
- **Improvement:** Alignment is substantively adequate, but a comparison or
  distinction should be more explicit.
- **Unverified:** Necessary configuration, unit, definition, or lifecycle
  context is unavailable.
