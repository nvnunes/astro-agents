# Decision Support

Assess whether each recorded research decision follows from the available
evidence, objectives, criteria, constraints, alternatives, and tradeoffs stated
or materially implicated by the research account.

## Inspect

Use the decision, its rationale and context, relevant evidence and uncertainty,
stated objectives and constraints, considered alternatives and tradeoffs, and
later material that changes the basis for the decision.

## Review Criteria

- Identify the decision and the objective it serves.
- Check whether material criteria and constraints are clear.
- Check whether the cited evidence bears on those criteria.
- Determine whether plausible alternatives and consequential tradeoffs were
  considered at the level needed for the decision.
- Consider whether unresolved uncertainty or later results materially weaken
  the recorded rationale.
- Keep the decision distinguishable from a proposal, provisional choice, or
  agent recommendation.

Review the decision in the context in which it was made, then consider later
retained material that may have changed its basis. Do not invent a replacement
decision.

## Exclusions

- You may report weak support or incomplete reasoning. Do not replace, reverse,
  or relabel the researcher's decision.
- Claim Support owns propositions, Methodological Validity owns method
  suitability, and Evidence Selection owns selection choices.
- A researcher-authorized follow-on operation owns any change to the decision
  or its rationale.

## Finding Guidance

- **Issue:** A recorded decision materially conflicts with its evidence,
  objectives, criteria, or constraints, or omits a consequential alternative or
  tradeoff.
- **Improvement:** The decision is defensible, but its rationale or comparison
  basis should be recorded more clearly.
- **Unverified:** The objectives, criteria, constraints, alternatives, or
  supporting evidence needed for review are unavailable.
