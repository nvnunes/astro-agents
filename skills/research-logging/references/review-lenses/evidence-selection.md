# Evidence Selection

Assess whether choices to include or exclude data, cases, runs, configurations,
and results are appropriate, transparent, and free from materially selective
treatment.

## Inspect

Use stated selection rules, included evidence, available inventories and
manifests, exclusions and their rationales, failed or negative cases,
experimental plans, scripts or configuration that implement selection, and
relevant surrounding entries.

## Review Criteria

- Determine whether inclusion and exclusion criteria are stated or otherwise
  recoverable.
- Assess whether those criteria fit the research question and were applied
  consistently.
- Check whether choices were independent of a preferred outcome or justified
  when outcome-dependent selection was necessary.
- Examine the treatment of failed, inconvenient, unfavorable, or anomalous
  cases.
- Consider whether omitted material could change the apparent result or
  conclusion materially.
- Check whether aggregation, weighting, and representative-case choices are
  disclosed at the level needed to understand the selection.

Compare the selected body with the available material only as far as that
universe can be established from retained inventories, artifacts, scripts, and
the research account. Do not infer selective omission solely from the absence
of unrecorded material.

## Exclusions

- Evidence Coverage owns whether the evidence body spans what the question
  requires.
- Statistical Validity owns statistical sampling, weighting, and inferential
  consequences beyond the selection decision itself.
- Implementation Fidelity owns whether code applies a described selection rule
  correctly.
- Mechanical validation does not judge why evidence was included or excluded.

## Finding Guidance

- **Issue:** Selection is materially biased, inconsistent, outcome-driven
  without justification, or hides consequential exclusions.
- **Improvement:** Selection is reasonable, but its criteria or exclusions
  should be more transparent.
- **Unverified:** The available universe, selection rule, or exclusion history
  cannot be established.
