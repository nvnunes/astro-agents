# Presentation Fidelity

Assess whether each figure, table, excerpt, caption, or derived statistic
communicates its underlying evidence faithfully, without materially misleading
choices or omissions.

## Inspect

Use the rendered presentation, caption and surrounding prose, retained data or
artifact from which it was produced, and relevant plotting, tabulation, or
formatting configuration and code.

## Review Criteria

- Check whether axes, scales, units, ranges, labels, legends, and encodings
  represent the underlying quantities intelligibly and without distortion.
- Identify normalization, aggregation, filtering, interpolation, and omitted
  cases that affect interpretation but are not apparent.
- Check whether comparisons use a fair and meaningful basis.
- Check whether the caption accurately describes what is shown and preserves
  material limitations.
- Determine whether visual or tabular choices create an impression stronger or
  different from the underlying evidence.

Inspect the rendered form rather than source markup alone. Use disposable
rendering or extraction when needed, and compare with underlying retained
material without regenerating the research artifact.

## Exclusions

- Mechanical validation owns whether supported values and structures match
  their declared retained sources.
- Result Derivation owns the substantive calculation that produced a retained
  result.
- Presentation Consistency owns unexplained drift across presentations.
- Research-Log Writing owns prose quality outside the presentation-specific
  question.

## Finding Guidance

- **Issue:** A presentation materially distorts, conceals, or overstates its
  evidence, or its caption or encoding supports a misleading interpretation.
- **Improvement:** The presentation is faithful but could make an important
  relationship, qualification, or encoding clearer.
- **Unverified:** The underlying artifact, rendering, or contextual information
  needed for judgment is unavailable.
