# Research-Log Conformance

Assess whether the account follows the semantic research-logging conventions
for section roles, entry boundaries, navigation, lifecycle state, and retained-
material placement.

## Inspect

Use the maintained summary, entry documents, research-log directory and
ownership structure, retained materials and their placement, cross-entry
references, lifecycle statements, and the current research-logging conventions.
Read only the applicable conventions in `references/file-entry.md`,
`references/file-entry-naming.md`, `references/file-entry-labels.md`,
`references/file-summary.md`, and `references/file-retention.md` for the
requested target.

## Review Criteria

- Check whether experimental, synthesis, and prose sections serve their defined
  semantic roles.
- Check whether standard blocks contain their assigned kinds of content without
  invented synonyms or incompatible section purposes.
- Assess entry and section boundaries against coherent research questions,
  comparisons, and decision contexts.
- Check whether summary navigation, entry links, related-entry references, and
  follow-ups expose the account usefully.
- Check whether correction, replacement, supersession, uncertainty, decisions,
  and lifecycle state follow the conventions.
- Assess whether retained material is owned, placed, and referenced at an
  appropriate entry or shared boundary.

Review against the current conventions while distinguishing deterministic
failures from semantic misuse. Do not turn stylistic preference into a
conformance requirement.

## Exclusions

- Mechanical validation owns deterministic checks of files, metadata, evidence
  associations, presentation templates, and structurally detectable failures.
  Use such failures as locators when useful; diagnose only the semantic misuse.
- Research-Log Writing owns communication quality within conforming surfaces.
- Scientific and evidentiary lenses own whether the recorded research is
  substantively sound.
- Preserve explicit researcher-approved structure and intent unless it
  conflicts concretely with the current research-log contract.

## Finding Guidance

- **Issue:** Semantic misuse of a section, block, boundary, lifecycle state,
  navigation surface, or retained-material location materially impairs the
  record or violates a required convention.
- **Improvement:** The account conforms, but a structural or navigational
  adjustment would make it more usable.
- **Unverified:** Intended lifecycle state, ownership, or a researcher-approved
  exception cannot be established.
