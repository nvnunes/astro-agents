# Research-Log Writing

Apply `$science-writing` together with the research-logging conventions. Assess
whether sections and the maintained summary communicate clearly and precisely,
organize the account around research questions and outcomes, avoid redundant
work narration, and form a coherent whole.

Use science-writing for scientific prose discipline and research-logging for
log-specific structure and conventions. Read
`references/research-log-writing.md` for the requested target. Keep findings
within this lens; do not silently add Claim Support, Claim Calibration,
Methodological Validity, or another review lens from the broader
science-writing guidance.

## Inspect

Use section prose and its applicable standard blocks, the maintained summary,
nearby figures and tables needed to understand the writing, and related sections
or entries needed to assess continuity and redundancy.

## Review Criteria

- Check whether each section makes its question, comparison, conditions, and
  outcome easy to understand.
- Check whether applicable standard blocks communicate their assigned content
  clearly, precisely, and proportionately.
- Include procedure and chronology only when needed to understand or reconstruct
  the research rather than as a work diary.
- Organize comparisons around research outcomes rather than a sequence of
  attempts.
- Consolidate or reference repeated explanations, results, and qualifications
  without losing necessary context.
- Assess whether sections and the maintained summary form a coherent account at
  their respective levels of detail.

Use the section as the primary writing unit and assess entry prose in that
context. Then assess whether the maintained summary and sections read as a
coherent log. Report recurring patterns together when that is more useful than
listing every local instance.

## Exclusions

- Research-Log Conformance owns whether standard surfaces and blocks serve their
  defined semantic roles; this lens owns the writing within them.
- Summary Fidelity owns whether the summary accurately represents entries.
- Claim Support and Claim Calibration own evidentiary warrant and the strength
  of claim wording.
- Cross-Entry Consistency and Presentation Consistency own factual and visual
  consistency.
- Review reports writing findings and does not rewrite the log until the
  researcher authorizes follow-on work.

## Finding Guidance

- **Issue:** Unclear, imprecise, fragmented, redundant, or work-log-oriented
  writing materially obscures the question, method, result, interpretation,
  decision, or current understanding.
- **Improvement:** Meaning is sound, but wording, organization, emphasis, or
  consolidation could make the account substantially easier to understand.
- **Unverified:** Missing context prevents a judgment about whether apparently
  unclear or redundant material serves a necessary purpose.
