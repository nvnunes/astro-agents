# Statistical Validity

Assess whether sampling, estimation, uncertainty treatment, aggregation, and
statistical inference support the reported conclusions.

## Inspect

Use data descriptions and retained summaries, sampling and grouping rules,
statistical models and code, uncertainty estimates, diagnostics, reported
effects, and claims that depend on statistical inference.

## Review Criteria

- Assess samples, observational units, independence assumptions, and groupings.
- Assess estimators, statistical models, tests, and aggregation rules against
  the data and question.
- Check the treatment of uncertainty, variability, missingness, censoring, and
  repeated measures.
- Consider multiplicity, model selection, and researcher degrees of freedom
  where they affect inference materially.
- Give effect sizes, intervals, diagnostics, and practical significance
  appropriate weight relative to thresholded significance.
- Keep conclusions within the populations and conditions represented by the
  analysis.

Follow each material statistical conclusion back through its estimator, data
structure, and uncertainty treatment. Disposable calculations may test
reasoning already present in retained material but must not become new research
evidence.

## Exclusions

- Methodological Validity owns the broader research design and scientific
  comparison.
- Numerical Validity owns computational stability, convergence, and precision.
- Result Derivation traces the calculation of a retained result without deciding
  whether the statistical inference is valid.
- Claim Calibration owns how inferential strength and uncertainty are expressed
  in prose.

## Finding Guidance

- **Issue:** A statistical assumption, estimator, uncertainty treatment,
  aggregation, or inference materially undermines a conclusion.
- **Improvement:** The inference is defensible, but an additional diagnostic,
  uncertainty description, or robust analysis would strengthen it.
- **Unverified:** Required data structure, sampling information, diagnostics, or
  statistical expertise is unavailable.
