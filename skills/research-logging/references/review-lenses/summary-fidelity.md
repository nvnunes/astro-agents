# Summary Fidelity

Assess whether the maintained summary accurately and currently represents the
detailed research record, including material qualifications, conflicts,
decisions, and superseded results.

## Inspect

Use the maintained summary, all entries relevant to its statements and
omissions, recorded decisions and follow-ups, lifecycle state, and linked
evidence needed to establish what the entries currently say.

## Review Criteria

- Trace each material summary statement to its supporting entries.
- Check whether the summary preserves qualifications, uncertainty, conflicts,
  and limitations that affect meaning.
- Identify later work that changes, narrows, contradicts, or supersedes
  summarized understanding.
- Check whether current decisions and material follow-ups are represented
  without promoting proposals or agent suggestions into researcher conclusions.
- Identify important current findings omitted while stale or rejected findings
  remain prominent.

Review in both directions: trace material summary statements back to entries,
then scan current entry conclusions and decisions for material understanding
that should appear in the summary.

## Exclusions

- Mechanical validation owns supported summary evidence references and exact
  presented values.
- Cross-Entry Consistency owns disagreements among entries. This lens owns the
  summary's representation of those entries and disagreements.
- Claim Support and Claim Calibration own the substantive warrant and wording
  of claims even when the summary transcribes entries faithfully.
- Research-Log Writing owns summary clarity and organization.

## Finding Guidance

- **Issue:** The summary is materially stale, inaccurate, incomplete,
  misleadingly selective, or omits a consequential qualification, conflict,
  decision, or supersession.
- **Improvement:** Current understanding is accurate but could be synthesized or
  linked more usefully.
- **Unverified:** Supporting entries or lifecycle information needed to
  evaluate a summary statement cannot be located or interpreted.
