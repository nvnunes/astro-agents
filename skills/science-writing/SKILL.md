# Science Writing

Use this skill for scientific and research prose.

Pair with `$technical-writing` for common prose discipline, then read `references/science.md` for scientific claim, evidence, methods, and uncertainty rules.

Preserve the author's technical intent. Tighten claims to match evidence, separate observation from inference, and make uncertainty or validation limits explicit. Do not add unsupported conclusions or smooth over methodological gaps.

When the prose is part of a research log, pair with `$research-logging` for entry structure, provenance, and retrieval conventions.
